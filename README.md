## 🇨🇳 中文

把神话、民间、古典、寓言或其他文化题材的创意、剧本、参考图，做成一部具有经典民族动画视觉语法的完整短片。默认分支是「复古中国民族动画数字复兴风」：工笔重彩人物、戏曲化姿态、敦煌飘带、青绿山水、平面赛璐珞分色、装饰性云火水纹，再用现代数字精修收口；也可切换到工笔重彩或水墨分支。流程共 20 步，先扩写并确认故事，锁定视觉方向，写出经用户确认的制作方案（含按总时长与复杂度自适应的镜头数量），再建风格圣经、角色三视图、场景与道具系统、分层调度与运动语法、分镜表与音频地图。资产锚点全部确认后才进入参考图、逐镜提示词、分镜视频、声音设计、混音与结构化复核，最终交付成片，并可按需保留可复用制作文档。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 三个视觉分支可选：复古中国民族动画数字复兴风（默认）、工笔重彩、水墨
- 20 步分阶段推进，故事、视觉方向、制作方案、资产锚点各自设确认节点，确认前不生成资产或视频
- 镜头数量按总时长与复杂度自适应：15–30 秒约 2–4 镜，中等 4–7 镜，复杂 7–12 镜
- 角色三视图＋角色—镜头映射，群像只设少量可区分配角，不反复复制主角
- 建立统一空间平面与摄影机路径：合页门绕真实门轴旋转，背景随镜头移动产生视差
- 打斗须走完「起势→攻击路径→接触点→受击反馈→反作用力→结果」闭环，攻击不能只改背景特效

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns a mythic, folkloric, classical, allegorical, or culturally rooted premise, script, or reference into a finished animated short with classic national-animation visual grammar. The default branch is a retro Chinese national-animation digital revival look — gongbi heavy-color figures, operatic poses, Dunhuang ribbons, blue-green landscapes, flat cel color separation, patterned cloud/fire/water motifs, closed out with modern digital polish — with separate gongbi and ink-wash branches. Across 20 steps it expands and confirms the story, locks visual direction, writes an approved production plan with duration- and complexity-aware shot counts, then builds a style bible, three-view character sheets, scene and prop systems, layered staging and motion grammar, and a shot table with an audio map. Only after every asset anchor is approved does it move into reference images, per-shot prompts, shot videos, sound design, mixing, and structured review — delivering the finished short plus reusable production documents when wanted.

### ✨ What it does

- Three visual branches: retro Chinese national-animation digital revival (default), gongbi heavy-color, and ink-wash
- Twenty staged steps with approval gates on story, visual direction, production plan, and asset anchors before any asset or video generation
- Duration- and complexity-aware shot counts: roughly 2–4 shots at 15–30s, 4–7 for medium, 7–12 for complex
- Three-view character sheets plus a character-to-shot map; crowd scenes use a few distinguishable supporting roles
- Unified spatial plans and camera paths — hinged doors rotate on real axes and backgrounds shift parallax as the camera moves
- Fights must close the loop from wind-up to attack path, contact point, hit feedback, reaction force, and result
